Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and deliver work you've asked for.

What we collect

  • Details you submit in the contact form: name, company, email, phone, country, service, budget, timeline and your message.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends — IP address, browser type and referring page — used for security and aggregate traffic counts.
  • Nothing else. This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare an estimate.
  • To deliver and support work you have engaged us for.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not add you to marketing lists because you sent an enquiry.
  • We do not use your project data to train models without a separate written agreement.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, export or delete the data we hold on you at any time through the contact form. We respond within 30 days. Where GDPR or comparable law applies, we act as data processor for client project data and as controller for our own enquiry records. EU and UK data-residency options are available on request.

Terms of service

Engagement

Work begins when you approve a written estimate and the first invoice clears. That estimate — its scope, timeline, price and assumptions — forms the agreement between us. Nothing said in a call or chat overrides it.

Scope changes

Anything outside the approved scope is quoted separately and only built once you approve it in writing. We will not silently absorb scope and we will not silently bill for it.

Your responsibilities

  • Provide content, access, credentials and decisions within agreed timeframes.
  • Ensure you own or are licensed to use any material you supply to us.
  • Nominate one person empowered to approve work.

Ownership

On full payment, all custom code, designs and documentation created for you become your property. Repositories, cloud accounts and domains are yours and are handed over at go-live. We retain ownership of our pre-existing frameworks, libraries and template source code, which are licensed to you under the terms below.

Confidentiality

We treat your business information, data and plans as confidential indefinitely. We sign your NDA on request, at no cost.

Liability

Our total liability for any claim is limited to the fees you paid us for the work in question. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, third-party service outages, or losses arising from changes made to delivered code by anyone other than us.

Third-party costs

Hosting, domains, SMS and WhatsApp credits, model API usage, app store fees, payment gateway fees and ad spend are billed by those providers to your own accounts and are not included in our fees.

Template licence agreement

These terms apply to every platform in the template marketplace.

  • Grants use of one hosted instance for one live commercial project while payments are current.
  • Source code is not delivered under a rental.
  • You may charge your own end users to use your instance.
  • Cancel at any time; access continues to the end of the paid month.
  • Payments made in the first six months are credited in full against a later purchase of the same platform.

Purchase licence

  • Grants perpetual use of the source code for one live commercial project.
  • You may modify the code without limit and deploy it to infrastructure you control.
  • Includes 12 months of updates and 6 months of engineering support.
  • You may not resell, sublicense, redistribute or open-source the code, in original or modified form.

Extended licence

  • Everything in the purchase licence, for unlimited projects.
  • Grants resale and white-label rights, including deploying instances for your own clients.
  • Includes 24 months of updates and 12 months of dedicated support.
  • You may not resell the source code itself as a competing template or script product.

Your data

On every licence, the data your platform holds is yours. You can export it in a standard format at any time, and we delete our copies on request after termination.

Refund policy

Template rentals

Cancel at any time. The current month is not refunded; billing simply stops at the end of it. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum term beyond the first month.

Template purchases

Refundable in full within 7 days of purchase if the source code has not yet been delivered and the platform has not been deployed. Once code is delivered, purchases are final — for the same reason no software vendor takes code back.

Custom projects

Milestones are refundable up to the point work on that milestone begins. If you cancel mid-project, you pay for completed milestones only and receive everything produced up to that point — code, designs and documentation. There is no exit fee.

Retainers and dedicated teams

Cancel with 30 days' written notice. The notice period is billable; nothing beyond it is. Unused change-request hours do not carry over and are not refunded.

How to request one

Send your invoice number through the contact form. Approved refunds are returned by the original payment method within 10 business days. Crypto payments are refunded in the same asset at the rate on the refund date.

  • Business hours: 09:00–18:00 local, Monday to Saturday, with overlapping shifts for the Americas, Europe, the Middle East and APAC.
  • First reply to new enquiries: within 12 business hours.
  • Written estimates: within 72 hours of receiving a brief.
  • P1 incident response: 15 minutes on Scale, 1 hour on Operate, 8 business hours on Care.
  • Uptime SLA: 99.98% on Scale, 99.9% on Operate, with service credits where we miss it.
