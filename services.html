Engagement models

Pick the commercial model that fits the risk

Fixed scope, flexible scope, or your own team on our payroll. Switch models mid-project without renegotiating the whole contract.

📦

Fixed price

Defined scope, defined budget, defined date. Best for websites, template deployments and well-understood modules.

  • Written scope & milestone plan
  • Payment tied to deliverables
  • Change requests quoted separately
⏱️

Time & materials

Evolving products where scope will change. You approve a sprint budget and see burn-down every week.

  • Two-week sprints with demos
  • Transparent time logs
  • Pause or scale at any sprint boundary
👥

Dedicated team

A pod that works only on your product, in your tools and your rituals. Monthly billing, no recruitment fees.

  • Named engineers, your time zone
  • Replace any member in 5 working days
  • You own all code and IP
