Billing schedule

Projects are milestone-billed: 40% to start, 30% at the design/staging sign-off, 30% on delivery. Retainers and dedicated teams are billed monthly in advance. Templates are paid once, upfront.

🌍

Currencies & taxes

Invoices issue in USD, EUR, GBP, AED or INR. Prices exclude VAT, GST and local taxes, which are added where legally required. Reverse-charge applies for EU/UK business customers with a valid VAT number.

🔒

Payment security

Card and wallet payments run through PCI-DSS Level 1 providers over the payment provider's own hosted checkout. We never ask for card numbers, CVV or banking passwords by email, chat or phone.

Pricing FAQ

The questions finance teams ask

Q

Is the estimate fixed or can it change?

+
  • Fixed-price estimates do not change unless you request scope that wasn't in the signed brief — and any change is quoted and approved by you in writing before it's built.
Q

Do you take a deposit?

+
  • Yes — 40% to begin on fixed-price work. Retainers and dedicated teams are billed a month in advance. Template rentals take the first month upfront.
Q

Can we pay in instalments?

+
  • Projects above $10,000 can be split across monthly instalments over the delivery period at no extra charge. Ask for it when you approve the estimate.
Q

Do you offer purchase orders and net-30 terms?

+
  • Yes, for enterprise clients and public-sector buyers after a short credit check. We can also onboard to your procurement or vendor portal.
Q

Are there any hidden or recurring costs?

+
  • Our fee is what's on the invoice. Third-party costs — hosting, domains, SMS credits, model API usage, app store fees, ad spend — are billed by those providers directly to your own accounts, and we tell you the expected monthly range before you commit.
Q

What happens if we cancel mid-project?

+
  • You pay for completed milestones only, and you receive everything produced up to that point — code, designs and documentation. No exit fee, no hostage-taking.
